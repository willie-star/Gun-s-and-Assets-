Responsible habits protect everyone

Firearm safety is an ongoing responsibility. Take an accredited safety course, follow the manufacturer's guidance, and obey all applicable laws.

Core safety principles

  • Treat every firearm as if it is loaded.
  • Keep it pointed in a safe direction and keep your finger away from the trigger until ready to fire.
  • Be certain of your target and what is beyond it.
  • When not in use, secure firearms so children and unauthorized people cannot access them. Follow local storage laws and qualified safety guidance.

If you encounter an unattended firearm, do not touch it. Move away and contact a responsible adult or local authorities.
