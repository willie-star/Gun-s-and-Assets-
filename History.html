Design, technology, and society

Firearms have changed alongside advances in materials, manufacturing, and engineering. Their history is also connected to sport, public life, conflict, and the ongoing work of building safer communities.

Look at the wider context

  • Study historical objects through reputable museums and archives.
  • Consider the social and human consequences behind technological change.
  • Remember that laws and traditions differ across places and time periods.

This page is a general introduction, not a technical guide. For practical questions, consult qualified professionals and authoritative local sources.
